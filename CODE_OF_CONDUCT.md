
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The BackInTime community aims to provide a respectful, welcoming, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in the project.

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected Behaviour

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving constructive, specific, and technically useful feedback.
- Welcoming questions from beginners.
- Discussing disagreements calmly and using evidence.
- Respecting different experiences and communication styles.
- Giving appropriate credit for other people's work.
- Protecting private information.
- Prioritizing user data safety when discussing backup and restore behaviour.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly disrupting technical discussions.
- Knowingly misrepresenting another person's work.
- Encouraging unsafe use of backup or restore functionality while concealing known risks.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and applying these expectations fairly.

They may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project maintainers privately using a contact method published in the repository.

Include relevant context or links when possible. Avoid publishing sensitive personal information.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be reviewed as promptly as practical and handled with discretion. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, communication channels, and situations where someone publicly represents the project.

## Acknowledgement

All project participants are expected to follow these community standards.
